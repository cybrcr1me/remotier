01 Positioning

Remotier keeps every host you own one keystroke away.

The problem

Credentials live in seven places. Hosts get retyped from memory. Config files drift between the laptop and the work machine. Every reconnect is small friction paid over and over.

The position

A single, quiet place for hosts, keys and identities, built for people who already know what SSH does. No onboarding tour. No feature carousel. It opens, it connects, it stays out of the way.
